S2He Lab Research

Selected Research Projects

Our research brings together human-centered AI, mobile and wearable sensing, and computational methods to understand health and behavior in everyday life and develop new approaches to sensing, assessment, and intervention.

Context-Aware Micro-Interventions for Social Anxiety

NIMH R01
Digital Mental Health Mobile Sensing Just-in-Time Intervention

We integrate mobile and wearable sensing with computational models of behavior and context to understand social anxiety in everyday life and deliver personalized interventions in moments of need.

Multimodal Biomarkers in Pediatric Neurologic Disease

Coulter Translational Research Partnership
Wearable Sensing Multimodal Biomarkers Motor Function Pediatric Health

Develop novel multimodal biomarkers to characterize disease in children with pediatric-onset multiple sclerosis (POMS), spinal muscular atrophy (SMA), and Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD), including wearable-derived measures of real-world motor function, ultrasound measures of muscle structure, and neuroimaging measures of central nervous system injury.

CommSense: Novel Methods to Measure Patient-Clinician Communication

Betty Irene Moore Fellowship for Nurse Leaders and Innovators
Clinical Communication Multimodal Sensing Human-Centered AI

Combine multimodal sensing and AI to characterize patient-clinician communication and design technologies that support high-quality, compassionate care.

SocialPulse: Real-World Social Interaction Sensing

NIMH R01
Wearable Sensing Social Behavior Privacy-Preserving AI

Develop privacy-preserving wearable sensing methods to detect and characterize an individual's social environment in everyday life and better understand their relationship to mental health and well-being.

WellConnected: Community Health & Well-Being

TYDE
Digital Health Social Connection Intervention Design

Develop personalized digital approaches to strengthen social connection and well-being by understanding how young people's behaviors, experiences, and social environments interact in everyday life.

Multiscale Modeling and Intervention for Improving Medication Adherence

NCI R01
Cancer Survivorship Medication Adherence Multimodal Sensing

Integrate longitudinal mobile, wearable, self-report, and medication-taking data to model the behavioral and contextual factors that shape adherence to endocrine therapy among breast cancer survivors.
